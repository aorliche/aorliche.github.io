Graphical abstract from the Intervention VAE paper

After more than a year in review, our paper titled Intervention on Modifiable Lifestyle and Physiological Factors via Variational Autoencoder Reveals Changes in Functional Connectivity-Mediated Risk for Alzheimer's Disease was finally published in the journal Imaging Neuroscience! It is currently (as of today) only available as the submission PDF but I hope the publication department will make an HTML version reasonably quickly.

The paper explores the role of counterfactual interventions on various biomarkers and the subsequent change in functional connectivity (FC) -mediated risk for Alzheimer's disease. The analysis utilizes patient populations from the UKB and HCP adult datasets using an FC-based AD risk model constructed from the ADNI dataset. The underlying VAE model is the same from my exploration of demographic confounds in the PNC dataset, applied to a new task.

The paper represents a large portion of my work during my year at Yale. I want to thank my advisor there, Dr. Yize Zhao, for her support and all her help with the revisions.
