A screenshot of my diet tracking/visualization app. Written in Golang using the Fyne toolkit.

After almost four months of dieting, I've succeeded in dropping my weight from around 165 to under 145 lbs, which was my goal at the start of the diet. During this time I've kept a food diary listing how many calories I ate, what exercises I did, the weight used, the number of sets/reps, important achievements, and many other random tidbits. Using the Fyne GUI toolkit I've build an app which reads my semi-structured diary and visualizes my progress over time in a calendar view.

I'm mostly happy with my weight, especially since I now have visible veins on my forearms and even my biceps. Yet I still feel like I could be leaner. Also, in spite of losing weight, I've recently achieved PRs on weighted dips (14 reps with 45 lbs loaded, up from 12) and one-arm hang duration (50s for my right and 48s for my left arm, up from 23/28). One-arm hang PRs can be rationalized by arguing that I made it easier by losing weight, but the dips PR is slightly harder to explain; am I getting stronger while losing weight?

What really opened up one-arm hangs for me was a realization: I could double my hang time by squeezing my gripping hand as hard as possible, preventing my grip from slowly sliding open. This is important in the one-arm hang when there is no ability to claw back the millimeters of lost finger contact. So it turns out one-arm hangs are a learned skill!
