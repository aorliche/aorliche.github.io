The Rogue Jedi comic folio one, available right now!

A project I have been working on the past half a year is now in a somewhat ready state. It's a comic book centered around a Rogue Jedi in a reimagining of the Star Wars Universe. The story of folio one follows the main character and her assistant as they land on an asteroid-turned-space spation and have an encounter with two other Jedi, along with their mysterious prisoner.

The main characters are all anthropomorphic animals, in part inspired by the legendary graphic novel Maus, although the subject matter of my comic is quite a bit lighter.
