Just recently I posted the initial version of a new web app I made: the Visual Quiz. It allows you to upload a labeled image, hide some of the terms, and turn it into a fill-in-the-blank quiz. Go here to see how to create and upload your own quiz. For its initial use case I'm using it to create Anatomy and Physiology quizzes to help me remember all the multitude of terms I need to remember for tutoring.

The inspiration came from a program I made about 10 years ago to quiz myself on the definition of terms in various subjects. Maybe I will implement that next.
